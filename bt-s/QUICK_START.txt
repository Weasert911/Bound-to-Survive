BOUND TO SURVIVE

BASIC CONTROLS

 MOUSE - Look Around & Aim Flashlight
 F key - Use Flashlight
 ESC - Pause Menu


HOW TO PLAY
 Survive for 90 seconds
 Use flashlight to stun the ghost and score points
 Avoid ghost attacks
 Score high

GHOST BEHAVIOR
 Teleports around the room
 Gets faster after each flashlight hit
 Attacks when close to you
 Makes sounds when nearby
