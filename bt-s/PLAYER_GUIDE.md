# 🎮 BOUND TO SURVIVE - PLAYER GUIDE

## 📋 GAME OVERVIEW
**Bound to Survive** is a 90-second horror survival game where you must endure against a relentless ghost. Your goal is to survive for the full 90 seconds while scoring as many points as possible by hitting the ghost with your flashlight.

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE
- **Survive** for 90 seconds
- **Score points** by hitting the ghost with your flashlight
- **Avoid** the ghost's attacks
- **Achieve** the highest score possible

## 🕹️ CONTROLS

### 🖱️ MOUSE CONTROLS
- **Look Around**: Move mouse to look in all directions
- **Flashlight Aim**: Mouse position determines flashlight direction
- **Sensitivity**: Adjust mouse sensitivity in game settings

### ⌨️ KEYBOARD CONTROLS
- **W/A/S/D Keys**: Move forward, left, backward, right
- **LEFT MOUSE BUTTON**: Activate flashlight (hold to keep on)
- **ESC Key**: Pause game / Access menu
- **R Key**: Restart game (if available)
- **M Key**: Return to main menu

### 🎮 GAMEPAD CONTROLS (if supported)
- **Left Stick**: Move character
- **Right Stick**: Look around / Aim flashlight
- **Right Trigger (R2)**: Activate flashlight
- **Start Button**: Pause game
- **Select Button**: Open menu

## 👻 GAME MECHANICS

### 💡 FLASHLIGHT SYSTEM
- **Cooldown**: Flashlight has a 3-second cooldown between uses
- **Stun Effect**: Hitting the ghost stuns it temporarily
- **Scoring**: Each successful hit gives 25-50 points
- **Strategy**: Time your flashlight uses to maximize stuns and points

### 👻 GHOST BEHAVIOR
- **Movement**: Ghost teleports between marked locations
- **Aggression**: Ghost becomes faster and more aggressive after each hit
- **Attacks**: Ghost can damage you when close
- **Sounds**: Ghost makes eerie sounds when nearby

### ⏱️ TIMER SYSTEM
- **90 Seconds**: You must survive this duration to win
- **Visual Feedback**: Timer changes appearance as time progresses
- **Final Countdown**: Last 10 seconds are most intense

## 🎯 SCORING & WINNING

### 🏆 SCORE TIERS
- **100+ Points**: PERFECT VICTORY! The ghost didn't stand a chance!
- **75+ Points**: EXCELLENT! You dominated the ghost!
- **50+ Points**: GREAT JOB! You survived with a solid score!
- **25+ Points**: GOOD WORK! You made it through alive!
- **Below 25**: You survived... barely! Better luck next time!

### 🎮 WIN/LOSE CONDITIONS
- **WIN**: Survive the full 90 seconds
- **LOSE**: Get caught by the ghost before time runs out

## 🎮 GAME FLOW
1. **Main Menu** → Start Game
2. **Intro Sequence** → Learn the story
3. **Gameplay** → Survive and score points
4. **Result Screen** → See your final score and performance
5. **Game Closes** → Automatic closure after results

## 💡 TIPS FOR SURVIVAL
- **Stay Mobile**: Keep moving to avoid ghost attacks
- **Listen Carefully**: Audio cues indicate ghost proximity
- **Time Your Flashlight**: Don't waste it, use it when ghost is close
- **Use Environment**: Hide behind objects when possible
- **Manage Stamina**: Don't exhaust yourself with constant movement

## ⚙️ SETTINGS & OPTIONS
- **Graphics**: Adjust quality settings for better performance
- **Audio**: Control volume levels for music and sound effects
- **Controls**: Remap keys if needed
- **Accessibility**: Adjust text size and contrast

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING
- **Game won't start?** Check if all files are properly loaded
- **Controls not working?** Verify input devices are connected
- **Performance issues?** Lower graphics settings
- **No sound?** Check audio device and volume settings

## 📊 PERFORMANCE GUIDE
- **Minimum Requirements**: 4GB RAM, Integrated Graphics
- **Recommended**: 8GB RAM, Dedicated GPU
- **Optimal**: 16GB RAM, High-end GPU
